Teaching a laptop to play Breakout from pixels with PPO

An agent sees only the screen: four stacked 84×84 grayscale frames. It has to learn to move the paddle and break bricks. It is trained with Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) on an 8-core laptop CPU with no GPU, for 10 million game frames (about 46 hours of play at 60 frames per second). The goal: a mean score of at least 300 over 10 full games (all 5 lives, unclipped score) on seeds it never trained on. Every run is tracked in MLflow; this page is rebuilt from that data every 15 minutes.

Training diagnostics from the MLflow run, exported to JSON

PPO's health signals per update: the approximate KL divergence between the old and new policy, the fraction of samples whose probability ratio was clipped, the policy's entropy (how random it still is), the value loss, and how well the critic explains the returns. Throughput is in agent steps per second (1 step = 4 frames).
