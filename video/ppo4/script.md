# Watching it learn

## Two million frames

After two million frames, the agent has barely started. It launches the ball and misses it almost at once. Two points, and the game is over.

## Ten million frames

At ten million frames, it follows the ball and returns it again and again. It clears the low rows one brick at a time. Fifty points.

## Twenty million frames

At twenty million frames, something new happens. After a long, patient start, the ball breaks through the wall. Above the bricks, it bounces on its own and clears the valuable top rows fast. Three hundred and seventy points.

## Forty million frames

The final agent plays the same game. It breaks through sooner, and it finishes with four hundred and four points.
